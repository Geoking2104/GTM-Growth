# Provider marks

`google-analytics.svg` is the Google Analytics product mark served by Google at
https://www.gstatic.com/analytics-suite/header/suite/v2/ic_analytics.svg and linked
from https://developers.google.com/analytics (retrieved 2026-11-16).

Used solely to identify the relevant data source. Other engine marks are reused
from `../ai-engines/`. Logos never indicate a connected account or endorsement.
All assets are served locally; displaying a mark does not contact its provider.
