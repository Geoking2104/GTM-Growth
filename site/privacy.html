Cookies and optional audience measurement

Each tool requires your consent. Reject both, accept both or choose each separately. Your browser remembers your decision for up to 180 days and asks again when it expires. Previous consent covering Clarity alone does not enable Google Analytics.

Google Analytics 4 through Google Tag Manager

With your consent, container GTM-MTLJ6WSM loads stream G-75DZ33J5QP to measure public website visits, page views and sessions. Google receives the public page path, browser information and pseudonymous identifiers. The _ga and _ga_75DZ33J5QP cookies distinguish visits and sessions. This site sets their maximum lifetime to 180 days. Advertising features and automatic detection of user-provided information are disabled. Google’s individual-data retention and aggregate reporting.

Microsoft Clarity

With your consent, Clarity can measure clicks and scrolling, generate heatmaps and replay sessions. The _clck and _clsk cookies link page views to a visitor and a session. Advertising storage is denied. Microsoft retains ordinary replays for 30 days and heatmaps and labelled or favourite sessions for up to nine months. Microsoft retention periods.

These tools cover public marketing pages, guides and this notice. The console, authentication, customer reports and payments are excluded. Withdrawing permission stops the corresponding measurement on subsequent visits and removes its accessible site cookies. Withdrawal does not retroactively delete data already transmitted.
