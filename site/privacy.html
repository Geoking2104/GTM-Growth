Data used

When you request a report, the form collects your name, work email, website and requirements to respond to your request. The free read sends the URL entered to the analysis service. Depending on the service configuration, the request may be stored on the server or forwarded to a form provider. An unsent request may be kept in your browser storage and retried later.

Console sign-in uses Keycloak. Connected-service data and permissions depend on access granted to your workspace. Session and configuration information is stored in your browser.

Audience measurement and choice

With your permission, the site loads Microsoft Clarity to measure site usage. You can accept or reject and change your choice at any time. The choice is stored in your browser for up to six months, if storage is available. Storage needed for operation, such as language and session information, is used independently of this choice.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, erasure or restriction of your data and, depending on the legal basis, object to processing or request portability. You may withdraw consent at any time where processing depends on it. Contact contact@kayroslab.com or write to the postal address above. You may also complain to the CNIL.

Retention and information still to be confirmed

The intended retention period for requests and accounts is one year from creation, renewable only at the client’s explicit request. Renewal and deletion mechanisms remain to be implemented; this period does not yet describe an automatic deletion process.

The identity of the data controller before incorporation, legal bases, retention for backups and records subject to legal obligations, actual recipients and any transfers outside the European Union remain to be confirmed for the live deployment. This page is therefore not yet a complete GDPR notice.
